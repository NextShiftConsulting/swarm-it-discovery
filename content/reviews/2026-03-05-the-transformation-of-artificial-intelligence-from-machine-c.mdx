Title: Tracing the Evolution of Artificial Intelligence: From Formal Algorithms to Cognitive Modeling

**Core Contribution**

This paper presents a comprehensive analysis of the transformation of artificial intelligence (AI) as a scientific field, charting its progression from traditional machine computing to more ambitious efforts in modeling human cognition. The key contribution of this work lies in its ability to identify the critical stages and paradigm shifts that have shaped the development of AI, providing valuable insights into the philosophical and methodological underpinnings of this rapidly evolving discipline.

The paper's central argument is that the rapid advancements in AI, driven by the increasing complexity of information processes and the broader context of digitalization, have necessitated a fundamental rethinking of the field's theoretical foundations. It demonstrates how AI has evolved from its early focus on solving well-defined computational problems to more ambitious attempts to capture the nuances of human thinking and reasoning, a shift that has profound implications for the future direction of the field.

**Technical Approach**

The paper adopts a historical and analytical approach to trace the evolution of AI, examining the key stages and paradigm shifts that have characterized its development. It begins by outlining the **computational approach**, which focused on formalizing and solving specific algorithmic problems, often drawing inspiration from the principles of information processing and computer science.

The analysis then delves into the **symbolic approach**, which sought to model human intelligence through the manipulation of symbolic representations and logical reasoning, aiming to capture the high-level, rule-based aspects of cognition. This was followed by the emergence of **learning-based approaches**, which leveraged statistical techniques and data-driven models to enable AI systems to acquire and adapt their capabilities through exposure to large datasets.

The paper also explores the current trends in AI, which are increasingly focused on **cognitive and neural network modeling**. These approaches seek to more closely emulate the complex neural architectures and information processing mechanisms of the human brain, with the goal of developing AI systems that can engage in more human-like reasoning, decision-making, and problem-solving.

**Key Results**

The paper's analysis of the transformative journey of AI highlights several key findings. First, it demonstrates the field's progression from narrow, well-defined computational tasks to more ambitious attempts to model the multifaceted nature of human cognition. This shift has led to the development of increasingly sophisticated AI architectures and techniques, such as deep learning, that can tackle more complex and open-ended problems.

Additionally, the paper underscores the growing recognition of the philosophical and methodological limitations of previous AI approaches, which often struggled to capture the nuances and context-dependent aspects of human intelligence. The emergence of cognitive and neural network modeling represents a concerted effort to address these shortcomings and develop AI systems that can better understand and interact with the world in a more natural and human-like manner.
