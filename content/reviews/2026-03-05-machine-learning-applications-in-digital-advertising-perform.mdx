# Machine learning applications in digital advertising performance optimization: A systematic literature review

**RSCT Certification:** κ=0.550 (pending) | **RSN:** 0.00/0.00/0.00 | **Topics:** AI Safety and Alignment

**Machine Learning Applications in Digital Advertising Performance Optimization: A Systematic Review**

**Core Contribution**

This systematic literature review provides a comprehensive analysis of how machine learning is transforming the digital advertising landscape. The study, covering the period from 2010 to 2025, aims to uncover the dominant machine learning techniques, performance metrics, industry applications, and key limitations in this rapidly evolving field. Given the staggering growth of digital advertising, which is expected to reach $798.7 billion globally by 2025, understanding the role of machine learning in optimizing advertising campaigns is of paramount importance.

The key finding of this review is the identification of several notable trends in the application of machine learning to digital advertising. These include the dominance of deep learning techniques, the convergence towards click-through rate (CTR) as a universal performance metric, the concentration of applications in the e-commerce sector, and the persistent challenge of data sparsity. The review also highlights critical gaps in addressing issues of fairness, sustainability, and robustness, which present valuable opportunities for future research and development.

**Technical Approach**

The authors employed the rigorous PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses) methodology to systematically identify, screen, and select relevant scientific literature. They applied a five-dimensional quality criteria (0-3 points per dimension) to evaluate the 42 "excellent" articles (13-15 points) included in the final analysis.

The review examines the distribution of machine learning techniques used in digital advertising optimization. The results show a clear dominance of deep learning approaches, with Deep Neural Networks (35.7%) and attention models (19.0%) being the most prevalent. This suggests a strong focus on developing sophisticated, end-to-end learning systems that can effectively capture the complex relationships between advertising parameters and user engagement.

In terms of performance metrics, the analysis reveals a remarkable convergence towards click-through rate (CTR) as the universal metric for evaluating advertising campaign effectiveness, used in 95.2% of the reviewed studies. This highlights the industry's emphasis on optimizing for immediate user engagement, which may have implications for the long-term sustainability and fairness of digital advertising practices.

The review also explores the industry applications of machine learning in digital advertising, with a notable concentration in the e-commerce sector (61.9% of studies), led by the prominent example of Alibaba (14.3% of studies). This suggests that leading e-commerce platforms have been at the forefront of leveraging machine learning to optimize their advertising strategies and improve customer engagement.

**Key Results**

The systematic review uncovered several key findings:

- **Dominance of Deep Learning**: Deep learning approaches, particularly Deep Neural Networks (35.7%) and attention models (19.0%), are the most widely adopted machine learning techniques in digital advertising optimization.
- **CTR as the Universal Metric**: Click-through rate (CTR) has emerged as the dominant performance metric, used in 95.2% of the reviewed studies.
- **E-commerce Concentration**: The majority of machine learning applications in digital advertising (61.9%) are concentrated in the e-commerce sector, led by Alibaba (14.3%).
- **Data Sparsity as a Limitation**: The review identified data sparsity as a fundamental limitation, affecting 59.5% of the studies.

**Significance & Limitations**

The insights from this systematic review hold significant implications for the future development of machine learning applications in digital advertising. The dominance of deep learning techniques underscores the industry's appetite for sophisticated, data-driven approaches that can navigate the complex relationships between advertising parameters and user engagement. However, the heavy reliance on CTR as the primary performance metric raises concerns about the potential trade-offs between short-term engagement and long-term sustainability or fairness.

Additionally, the concentration of machine learning applications in the e-commerce sector suggests a need to explore the transferability of these techniques to other industries, where the dynamics and user preferences may differ. The persistent challenge of data sparsity also highlights the importance of developing robust, sample-efficient machine learning algorithms that can overcome the limitations of sparse or biased datasets.

Future research in this field should aim to address the critical gaps identified in the review, such as the lack of attention to fairness, sustainability, and robustness. Developing equity-aware frameworks, exploring the environmental and societal impact of digital advertising, and enhancing the overall resilience of machine learning models will be crucial for the long-term viability and responsible development of this rapidly evolving industry.
