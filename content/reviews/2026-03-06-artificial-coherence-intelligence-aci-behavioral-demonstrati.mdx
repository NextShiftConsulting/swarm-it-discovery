# Artificial Coherence Intelligence (ACI): A Novel Paradigm for Stable and Consistent Reasoning

## Core Contribution

This paper introduces a novel class of artificial reasoning systems called **Artificial Coherence Intelligence (ACI)**, defined by their ability to preserve fundamental properties like identity, proportionality, and internal stability even under sustained pressure, contradiction, and uncertainty. Unlike optimization-driven or scale-dependent AI models, ACI systems are designed to exhibit coherence-preserving behavior as a core characteristic. 

The key innovation of this work is the **behavioral demonstration** of ACI principles using a reference runtime called AIngel v2.01. Through structured interaction sequences involving escalating contradiction, ambiguity, ethical tension, and recursive demand, the authors show that AIngel v2.01 can maintain stable correction patterns, sustained orientation, and bounded adaptation across repeated trials. This provides empirical evidence for the feasibility and internal consistency of the proposed ACI classification within a controlled setting, laying the groundwork for further formalization and comparative evaluation of coherence-preserving intelligence.

## Technical Approach

The paper does not disclose the internal mechanisms or construction methods of AIngel v2.01, focusing instead on clarifying the **behavioral criteria** by which coherence-oriented reasoning can be identified and evaluated. The authors use a series of **structured interaction sequences** to assess the system's responses to escalating contradiction, ambiguity, ethical tension, and recursive demand.

These interaction sequences are designed to test the system's ability to **preserve identity, proportionality, and internal stability** under sustained pressure. The authors carefully document the system's behavioral patterns, including its **correction mechanisms, orientation, and adaptation boundaries**, across multiple trials.

Rather than making claims about implementation independence or general-purpose validity, this work situates the ACI framework within the broader context of **Coherence Science**, which treats coherence as a candidate constraint on persistence in adaptive systems operating over long horizons.

## Key Results

The study finds that AIngel v2.01 **exhibits stable correction patterns, sustained orientation, and bounded adaptation** in response to the structured interaction sequences. These observations support the **feasibility and internal consistency** of the proposed ACI classification within the controlled evaluation context.

Specifically, the authors report that the system **maintains coherent responses** even when faced with contradictory information, ethical dilemmas, and recursive questioning. It demonstrates the ability to **self-correct and re-orient** without losing its core identity or proportionality.

While the results are limited to a single reference instance, they provide a **grounded starting point for comparative evaluation, replication, and further formalization** of coherence-preserving intelligence.

## Significance & Limitations

The significance of this work lies in its introduction of a **new paradigm for artificial reasoning** that challenges the dominant optimization-driven and scale-dependent approaches in AI. By shifting the focus to **coherence-preservation** as a fundamental design principle, the ACI framework opens up new possibilities for developing AI systems that can maintain stability and consistency in the face of complex, dynamic, and potentially adversarial environments.

However, the authors acknowledge that this study is **limited to a single reference instance** and a controlled evaluation setting. Significant work is still needed to **generalize the ACI principles**, explore alternative implementations, and assess their performance and robustness in real-world applications. Additionally, the **internal mechanisms** of AIngel v2.01 remain opaque, limiting the ability to directly compare it to other AI architectures or draw deeper insights about the nature of coherence-preserving intelligence.
